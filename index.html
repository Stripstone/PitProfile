What is a pit profile?

A soil pit profile is a vertical cross-section of the ground, divided into horizons — layers that differ in color, texture, and composition. Field scientists dig pits and record each horizon to understand soil history, archaeology, and land use.

PitProfile turns your field notes into a clean visual column — instantly.

Munsell soil color

Each horizon has a Munsell color code — a scientific standard used by soil scientists worldwide. It has three parts:

  • Hue — the color family (e.g. 10YR = yellow-red)
  • Value — lightness, 2 (very dark) → 8 (very light)
  • Chroma — saturation, 1 (dull) → 8 (vivid)

Series & Profiles

A series is any named grouping of profiles — a survey series, an excavation unit, a trench, or any sequence that makes sense for your project.

Each series contains as many profiles as you need. Switch between series instantly with the selector in the toolbar.

Your workflow

Recording a pit takes under a minute:

  1. Select or create a series, then navigate to a profile
  2. Tap the horizon letter (A/B/C/E) and dial in the Munsell color
  3. Type the depth in cm — the profile renders live
  4. Tap ↺ to log artifact finds — see the next slide

Data saves automatically to your browser. Connect a team code to sync across devices.

Artifacts & labels

Each horizon can carry an artifact find. Tap ↺ on any row to cycle through types: Lithic, Ceramic, Bone, Shell, Metal, and more. Double-tap ↺ to clear it.

The artifact appears as an icon on the profile at the correct depth. Tap the icon to toggle labels on and off.

Tap a label to type a note — a quick description, find number, or any field annotation. Press Enter or click away to save.
